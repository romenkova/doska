# Cards

A card body is Markdown. Click the text to edit it.

- `/` opens the command menu
- `[[12]]` links another card
- `#` adds a tag, `@` mentions someone
- `- [ ]` makes a task, the card shows how many are done
- `-cut-` on its own line ends the preview on the board
- Files attach by dropping, pasting, or the paperclip in the card header

All shortcuts are under the `?` button on a card.
