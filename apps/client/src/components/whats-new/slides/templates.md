# Templates

Any card or board can now be a template. Pick **Add to templates** in its menu.

- **Cards**: create one from a template with the button next to `+` in any column
- **Boards**: create one through **New board** on the **Templates** page in the sidebar
- Copies are made as-is, except assignees and attachments, which are left out
- Removing a template only removes the mark. The card or board itself stays

Tip: keep your templates in a dedicated folder, so they're harder to delete by accident.
